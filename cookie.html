Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2025 | Last Updated: February 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Genesis website (stableversionapps.github.io/genesis-personal) uses cookies and similar technologies. We believe in being transparent about how we interact with your browser.

Note: The Genesis mobile application does not use cookies. This policy applies only to our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device to remember preferences, understand usage patterns, and improve your experience. They are a standard part of how the web works.

2. How We Use Cookies

Our website is a static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. We use cookies sparingly and only when necessary:

Essential Cookies

These are required for basic website functionality, such as page navigation and access to secure areas. They do not collect personal information and cannot be disabled without affecting site functionality.

Preference Cookies

These remember your settings (such as theme preferences) to provide a more personalized experience. They are set only when you make a choice and do not track you across other websites.

Analytics Cookies

If enabled, these help us understand how visitors interact with our website — such as which pages are most visited — so we can improve content and navigation. Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized. No non-essential analytics cookies are set without your prior consent.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may set its own cookies as part of its infrastructure. We do not control GitHub's cookies. For more information, please review GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We do not use advertising cookies or share cookie data with advertisers.

4. Your Consent & Choices

In accordance with GDPR, DPDPA, and other applicable privacy regulations:

  • No non-essential cookies are set without your prior consent.
  • You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.
  • You can withdraw consent by clearing cookies and revisiting your preferences.
  • Rejecting non-essential cookies will not affect the core functionality of our website.

5. How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View and delete existing cookies.
  • Block all cookies or specific types.
  • Set preferences for specific websites.

Please refer to your browser's help section for specific instructions.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for regulatory reasons. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
