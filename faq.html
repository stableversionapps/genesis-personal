Frequently Asked Questions

How does Genesis Essential Assistant use my data?

Genesis only collects data essential for providing productivity tools, like account information and preferences. We will never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. For sensitive information, advanced encryption standards are used.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored on secure, cloud-based servers located in the regions specified in our Privacy Policy. We comply with data residency laws where required.

Does Genesis share my data with third parties?

Genesis does not sell your data. We only share data with third-party services necessary to deliver specific app features (like syncing with calendar providers), and only after your explicit consent.

Can I export or delete my data?

Absolutely. You can export your data at any time from your account settings. To delete your data, please visit account settings or contact support@genesisapp.com.

How can I delete my Genesis account?

You can delete your account at any time by going to Settings > Account and clicking Delete Account. This will permanently remove your personal data from our servers within 30 days. If you need help, contact support@genesisapp.com.

How do I control notifications and privacy settings?

You have full control over your notification and privacy preferences in the app. Go to Settings > Privacy to update your choices at any time.

What happens if I connect an integration (like Google Calendar)?

Genesis will only access data required to enable the integration's features. We do not store third-party credentials, and you can disconnect integrations at any time.

Who can see my information?

Only you (and, for collaborative features, people you explicitly invite) can access your private content. Our staff can access your data only in order to provide support, and only with your consent.

Do you use cookies or tracking technologies?

We use only essential cookies to keep you signed in and to deliver core functionality. We do not use third-party analytics or ad trackers.

Need more info? Read our Privacy Policy, or contact support@genesisapp.com.
