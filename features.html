Features

Everything you need to stay organized, productive, and in control — with your privacy always protected.

Notes & Documents

Create rich text notes, attach photos and files, scan documents with your camera, and keep everything organized in one place.

Tasks & Reminders

Manage task lists with time-based and location-based reminders. Get notified when you arrive at a place or when a deadline approaches.

On-Device AI Assistant

Chat with a powerful AI assistant that runs entirely on your device. No internet needed, no data sent to the cloud — your conversations stay private.

Voice Transcription

Record voice notes and get instant transcription powered by on-device Whisper AI. Works completely offline.

Privacy-First Security

Your data is encrypted with SQLCipher, protected by biometric authentication, and sandboxed on your device. No servers, no tracking, no compromises.

Works 100% Offline

Every feature in Genesis works without an internet connection. Your productivity is never dependent on connectivity.
