By using Genesis, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ 1. OVERVIEW & CORE PRINCIPLES ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ Genesis is built with a LOCAL-FIRST, PRIVACY-FIRST architecture: ✓ ALL data is stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite databases ✓ NO cloud synchronization (unless you explicitly enable it in future updates) ✓ NO data collection or transmission to external servers ✓ NO tracking, analytics, or behavioral profiling ✓ NO advertisements or third-party data sharing ✓ AI processing happens ON-DEVICE using local AI models Your data is YOUR data. DATA COLLECTION & STORAGE ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ 2.1 WHAT DATA IS STORED LOCALLY The App stores the following types of data locally on your device: • Notes & Documents: Text notes, document attachments, photos, and files you create or import • Tasks & Reminders: Task lists, time-based reminders, location-based reminders, and geofences • Voice Recordings: Audio recordings for voice notes (stored locally, never transmitted) • AI Conversations: Chat history with local AI assistant (processed on-device) • User Settings: App preferences, theme settings, notification preferences • Location Data: Geographic coordinates for location-based reminders (stored locally only) All this data is stored in: • SQLite databases (encrypted with SQLCipher when enabled) • Device file system (for media attachments) • Secure keychain/keystore (for encryption keys and sensitive settings) 2.2 WHAT DATA IS NOT COLLECTED The App DOES NOT collect, transmit, or store: ✗ Personal identifiable information (PII) beyond what you enter ✗ Device fingerprints or tracking identifiers ✗ Usage analytics or behavioral data ✗ Location history (beyond what's needed for active geofences) ✗ Voice recordings or transcriptions on external servers ✗ AI conversation history on external servers ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ 3. DEVICE PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ The App requests the following device permissions to provide its features. All permissions are used exclusively for local processing and functionality. 3.1 CAMERA PERMISSION (android.permission.CAMERA) Used for: • Scanning documents using your device camera • Capturing photos to attach to notes and documents • Adding images to your personal content How your data is handled: ✓ Photos are stored locally on your device ✓ Images are NOT uploaded to any server ✓ No image analysis happens on external servers ✓ You control when the camera is activated 3.2 MICROPHONE PERMISSION (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) Used for: • Recording voice notes and audio memos • Real-time voice transcription for AI assistant conversations • Voice input for note-taking How your audio is handled: ✓ Audio recordings are stored locally on your device ✓ Voice transcription happens ON-DEVICE using Whisper AI models ✓ No audio is transmitted to cloud services ✓ You control when recording starts and stops 3.3 LOCATION PERMISSIONS (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) Used for: • Location-based reminders (e.g., "Remind me when I reach the grocery store") • Geofencing to trigger notifications when entering/leaving places • Displaying location context in notes and documents How your location is handled: ✓ Location data is stored locally only ✓ Used exclusively for reminder triggers ✓ Background location access is ONLY used for active geofences ✓ No location tracking or history logging ✓ No sharing with third parties or advertising networks 3.4 STORAGE PERMISSIONS (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Used for: • Reading and saving document attachments • Importing files and images • Exporting notes and data backups How your files are handled: ✓ Only files you explicitly select are accessed ✓ No scanning or indexing of your device storage ✓ Files remain on your device 3.5 NOTIFICATION PERMISSIONS (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Used for: • Displaying time-based reminder notifications • Location-based reminder alerts • Task due date notifications How notifications work: ✓ All notifications are generated locally on your device ✓ No push notification servers involved ✓ You control notification settings in app preferences 3.6 FOREGROUND SERVICE & WAKE LOCK Used for: • Running background location monitoring for geofences • Ensuring reminders trigger reliably • AI model processing during conversations
